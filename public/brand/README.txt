BITE AND MELT brand images
==========================
Drop your files here (then reload the app; no restart needed):

  logo.png      your Bite and Melt logo (PNG, square or wide, at least 512 px; transparent background is fine)
  favicon.png   optional browser-tab icon (square PNG, 256 px or more). If missing, logo.png is used.

You can also upload the logo from the app: Settings > Cafe logo (this takes priority over logo.png).
Until a logo is added, the built-in burger logo is shown.
